E-ink seasonal screens: mockups

Each screen is the real 1200×825 Inkplate canvas, with the same fixed weather header and spa footer as the current firmware. Only the middle changes. Numbers are sample data. QR codes for real pages scan; gray-framed ones are placeholders until a link is chosen.

Ski report: nearby areas

Shown Nov–Apr, only when at least one area is open today or opens within 3 days (snowmaking alone doesn't count).

Data: each resort's own report page, scraped by Home Assistant (Bretton Woods, Cannon), with SnoCountry as a fallback.

Ski detail: one resort with its trail map

Rotates through open resorts. The map is a dithered 1-bit image stored on the device; open trails are listed beside it.

Snowmobiling

Shown Dec–Mar, when there's at least 6″ of snow on the ground at the house for 3 days and local trails are reported open.

Rentals: NH Fish and Game rental agents list. Registration: NH Fish and Game.

Santa's Village

Shown only when the park is open today or within the next 3 days.

Dates: santasvillage.com 2026 calendar (Christmastime Saturdays Nov 7–Dec 19 plus Fri Nov 27, 11–7; Sundays Nov 22–Dec 20, 11–6; New Year's Eve 4–9).

Hiking

Shown May–Oct when there's no snow on the ground. Trail list is curated in Home Assistant; one QR code per trail.
